# Privacy Policy for Velvet YouTube Robot

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Velvet YouTube Robot is a private tool used only by Velvet Rose Noir Media to manage its own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

## What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, given through Google sign-in, the tool uses YouTube API Services to read and update the owner's own YouTube channels: video titles, descriptions, tags, privacy and schedule settings, thumbnails, captions and playlists. It does not collect any data about viewers or other YouTube users.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to manage those channels. The work is carried out on the owner's behalf by the owner's AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic). The data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising.

## Where it is kept

The sign-in tokens are kept privately in the owner's own storage. Nothing else is stored.

## Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
